This notice explains how **[BUSINESS NAME]** ("we") handles personal data in
connection with AnalytIQ Inventory & POS, the checkout website and licence

supervisory authority.

## 1. Data inside the app

1.1 The desktop app stores everything in a database file on your computer:
products, sales, customers, suppliers, users and settings. We have no remote
access to it and receive no copy of it.

1.2 You are the controller of that data. You decide who uses the app, what
customer details you record and how long you keep them. Use Windows user
accounts and app roles to limit access, and take regular backups.

1.3 Licence activation is offline: the app checks the key's signature using a
public key built into the software. It does not contact us to activate, and
only a random installation id and activation date are stored locally.

## 2. Data we handle when you buy

2.1 Checkout on [WEBSITE DOMAIN] collects your name, business name, email,
phone and plan. Payment is processed by **Paystack**; card details go directly
to Paystack and never reach us. See Paystack's privacy policy.

2.2 We keep the order reference, contact details and the issued licence key so
we can deliver the key, provide support and meet tax/accounting obligations.
Records are kept for **[RETENTION PERIOD]** and then deleted or anonymised.

2.3 Legal basis: performance of our contract with you (delivery and support)
and compliance with Nigerian tax and accounting law.

## 3. Optional integrations you enable

3.1 **WhatsApp alerts** send message text to Meta's WhatsApp Cloud API using
credentials you supply.

3.2 **AI message rewriting** sends message text to the OpenAI-compatible
endpoint you configure.

3.3 **Branch sync** sends your chosen business records through the relay you
configure. Credentials for all three are stored only in your local database.

3.4 These features are off until you enable them. If your messages contain
customer personal data, make sure you have a lawful basis before sending them.

## 4. Your rights

Under the NDPA you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of
personal data we hold about you, object to processing, or request a copy in a
portable format. Email **[EMAIL]**; we respond within the timeframe the NDPA
sets. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

## 5. Security

We keep the licence signing key offline, use HTTPS on the checkout site, and
limit internal access to order records. No system is perfectly secure; report
suspected breaches to **[EMAIL]** promptly.
