We sell software licences, delivered instantly as a licence key.

## 1. Try before you buy

Every installation includes a free 14-day trial with the same features as the
paid product. Please use the trial to confirm the software works with your
scanner, receipt printer and daily sales flow before paying.

## 2. When we refund

2.1 **Not activated:** cancel within **7 days** of purchase for a full refund.

2.2 **Cannot be activated:** if your key does not activate and we cannot fix it
within **5 business days**, you may choose a replacement key or a full refund.

2.3 **Defective software:** if a material defect is reported within **30 days**
of purchase and we cannot repair it in a reasonable time, you may choose a
replacement or a refund, in line with your consumer rights.

## 3. When we do not refund

3.1 After a key has been activated, except under clauses 2.2 and 2.3.

3.2 Where a key has been shared or used at more locations than the plan allows.

3.3 For hardware, Windows or network problems outside the software (we will
still help you diagnose them).

## 4. How to request

Email **[EMAIL]** with the order reference (from your Paystack receipt), the
business name and the reason. We reply within **2 business days**. Approved
refunds go back through Paystack to the original payment method, normally
within **5–10 business days** depending on your bank.

## 5. Chargebacks

Please contact us first — most issues are resolved the same day. If you raise a
chargeback without contacting us, the associated licence key is suspended while
the bank investigates.
